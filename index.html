About me

I am an astrophysics researcher and scientific software developer at INAF — Osservatorio Astrofisico di Arcetri in Florence.

My work sits at the intersection of adaptive-optics instrumentation and data-driven astrophysics: I develop control and optical-calibration software for the ESO ELT M4 deformable mirror and its Optical Test Tower, I lead the metrology-bench integration of the Petalometer segmented mirror, and as PI of BINCAT I am building a catalog of close binary stars to evaluate and optimise AO performance for the ELT era.

I graduated in Astronomy & Astrophysics from Sapienza University of Rome (110, thesis on globular-cluster dynamics with Gaia DR3), and I care deeply about modern software practice — Git/CI-CD, GPU computing, and local-LLM agentic workflows — applied to scientific instrumentation.

2025 — presentMetrology

Petalometer bench

Integration & alignment · INAF Arcetri

Metrology-bench integration for the PetalMirror (PTM): a 6-segment actuated mirror with 3 piezo actuators per segment providing piston, tip & tilt control.

  • Fizeau interferometer — absolute tip/tilt measurement and correction.
  • Sensor for Phase Lag (SPL) — differential piston vs. the central fixed segment, on a 6 mm laser focus centred on the gap.
  • AttoCube interferometer — absolute height in a 6-laser configuration covering all segments.
SPIE 2026 paper (DOI)

Past research

Globular-cluster dynamics with Gaia DR3

Master's thesis · 2022 — 2023

Extraction of the effective gravitational potential of globular clusters from phase-space data and internal velocity distributions — the first observational counterpart to its theoretical derivation.

SZ-signal simulations for MUSTANG-2

MSc research experience · 2022

Simulation of point-like and extended Sunyaev–Zel'dovich signals from the hot gas of merging galaxy clusters, observed through a CCD array similar to the GBT's MUSTANG-2 instrument, with filtering optimisation (with Prof. E. Battistelli's team, Sapienza).
